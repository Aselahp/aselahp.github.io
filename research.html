Research

I develop methods that improve how graph neural networks represent structure, propagate information and model network processes.

Structure and expressivity

I study graph partitioning, invariant representations and propagation methods that help graph neural networks capture structural interactions and distinguish graph patterns.

Information flow and adaptive depth

My work examines adaptive propagation depth, curvature and information flow, including the echo chamber effect and learning under varying homophily.

Dynamic processes on graphs

I develop graph-based models for processes that unfold over networks, with work on influence maximization and epidemic time series prediction.

Scientific machine learning

I apply graph learning to gene prioritization and molecular property prediction, and study how scientific constraints can guide predictive models.
