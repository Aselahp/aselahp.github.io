About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Melbourne, working with Prof. Saman Halgamuge. I received my PhD from the Australian National University, where I was supervised by the late Prof. Qing Wang, Dr. Ahad N. Zehmakan and Dr. Asiri Wijesinghe.

My research focuses on graph neural networks, machine learning theory and network science. I study how graph models represent structure, how information propagates through networks, and how these models can describe dynamic processes. My applied work includes gene prioritization and molecular property prediction.

Previously, I taught at the Australian National University and the University of Ruhuna, and worked in software engineering at Codegen International.
